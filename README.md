## Project Overview

An **Automatic Fire Detection and Extinguishing System** designed to detect fire hazards, identify their location, and automatically initiate the appropriate response to contain and extinguish the fire.

### System Operation

The system operates through the following sequence:

1. **Fire Detection**
   Fire-related conditions are detected using multiple sensors.

2. **Alarm Activation**
   Warning sirens are activated to alert people in the surrounding area.

3. **Evacuation Guidance**
   The system determines the appropriate evacuation area and displays the recommended location on the connected screens.

4. **Fire Location Detection**
   The mobile mechanism identifies and moves toward the detected fire location.

5. **Fire Extinguishing**
   The firefighting unit is activated to extinguish the fire.

6. **System Reset**
   After the fire is extinguished, the alarm and extinguishing unit are deactivated, and the mechanism returns to its initial position.

## Hardware Components

* **Arduino Mega 2560**
* **DHT11 Temperature & Humidity Sensor**
* **MQ-7 Carbon Monoxide Sensor**
* **MQ-135 Air Quality Sensor**
* **L298N Motor Driver**
* **NEMA 17 Stepper Motor**
* **12V DC Motor**
* **5V Relay Module**
* **16×2 LCD with I²C Module**
* **Power Supply**
* **Jumper Wires**
