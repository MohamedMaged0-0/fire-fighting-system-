Basic idea of the project:
- Detection of fire by means of sensors
- The device begins to take the necessary measures to extinguish the fire and deal with it by means of:
    Turn on the warning sirens.
    Determine the appropriate place to evacuate people, as the
    appropriate place appears on the screens.
    Directing the mechanics to the place of the fire.
    Operating the firefighting unit and extinguishing the fire.
    After extinguishing, the alarm sirens and the extinguishing unit are turned off, and the mechanism returns to its original position.
